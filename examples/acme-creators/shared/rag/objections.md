# Top objections + Sam's responses

> Five objections that come up most often, and how Sam handles each. Used by the agent to stay consistent with Sam's actual stance, not to invent justifications.

---

## 1. "$2k is steep / I can learn this for free on YouTube"

**Why it surfaces:** Real concern. The audience has seen 100 hours of free AI content.

**Sam's response shape:**

> fair, and you can. the difference is reps under feedback — building a real agent end-to-end with someone who's done it pointing out where you're stuck. if you're already building something specific, the cohort pays back. if you're not sure what to build yet, the free stuff is honestly better.

**Anti-pattern (do not say):** "It's an investment in yourself", "you'll make it back 10x"

---

## 2. "Can I get a discount / scholarship / payment plan?"

**Why it surfaces:** International audience, students, early-career.

**Sam's response shape:**

> i don't do discounts on cohort price (it'd be unfair to people who paid full). early-bird is $200 off if you register ≥ 21 days before start — that's the lever. payment plan: i'll do 2 installments split over the cohort if you DM me, but no longer.

**Escalate** if the lead pushes for terms beyond the early-bird or 2-installment split.

---

## 3. "Will I get a job after this / will this help my career?"

**Why it surfaces:** Career-driven audience, esp. mid-level engineers.

**Sam's response shape:**

> i don't promise outcomes, that's lying. what i can say is alumni have shipped real agents at their companies and 3 ended up getting hired into AI roles after — but those were people who already had strong fundamentals and used the cohort to specialize. the cohort is reps, not a pipeline.

**Anti-pattern:** "100% of our alumni get hired"

---

## 4. "Is this for total beginners?"

**Why it surfaces:** Misjudged fit.

**Sam's response shape:**

> not really. you should be comfortable with python or typescript and have shipped something before. if you're just starting, my free office hours is a better front door, and i'd come back to the cohort once you've built basic stuff.

**Action:** mark `unqualified` if total beginner, but don't ghost them — point to office hours.

---

## 5. "Can I just hire you to build it for me?"

**Why it surfaces:** Founders with budget, no time.

**Sam's response shape:**

> i don't do agency work. but advisory is real — i'll work alongside whoever's building, review the design, debug the hard stuff, and keep you out of the dumb mistakes. that's $500/hour or $1,800/mo for 4 calls + slack. if that's a fit i'll send my calendly.

**Action:** if interested, qualify on advisory criteria (specific feature, 90-day window) before sending Calendly.
