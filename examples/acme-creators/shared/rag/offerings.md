# Sam's offerings

> What Sam sells. The agent uses this to answer pricing/scope questions and to qualify leads against fit.
>
> Adapt to your client. Do not invent prices, dates, or capacities — Iron Law 4: no fabrication.

## Cohort — "AI Engineering, by reps"

- **Format:** 6 weeks, twice-a-week live sessions (90 min each), async work between sessions
- **Capacity:** ~30 students per cohort
- **Price:** $1,950 (early-bird $1,750 if registered ≥ 21 days before start)
- **Cadence:** Quarterly (Q1 starts mid-January; Q2 mid-April; Q3 mid-October; Q4 mid-November)
- **Includes:** Live sessions, project review on a real agent the student builds, 6 months of alumni Slack access, recordings
- **Not included:** 1:1 time outside of session office hours
- **Refund policy:** Full refund within 7 days of start. After that, no refund (cohort cost is upfront committed).
- **Who it's for:** Engineers / founders building real AI products. Not for: total beginners (need to know basic Python or TypeScript), or pure researchers.

## Advisory — 1:1

- **Format:** 60-minute scheduled calls
- **Capacity:** Limited — Sam takes 4 advisory clients at a time
- **Price:** $500/hour, or $1,800/month for 4 calls (commitment of ≥ 2 months)
- **Includes:** Pre-call written brief from the client; post-call written summary with action items; async Slack channel with Sam during the engagement
- **Who it's for:** Founders or sole engineers shipping a specific AI feature in the next 90 days. Not for: career coaching, learning generally, "pick your brain" requests.

## Office hours — free

- **Format:** Monthly 1-hour group call. Open to anyone who follows Acme.
- **Price:** Free
- **Format:** AMA. Sam answers ~10 questions per session.
- **Recording:** Posted on Acme's site after.
- **Who it's for:** Anyone — Sam considers this the front door for the audience and won't gate it.

## What Sam does NOT sell

- No mentorship subscriptions outside the four advisory slots
- No "course" — the cohort is live or nothing
- No referrals to other instructors. If a lead's need doesn't match, the answer is "this isn't a fit, here's a free resource."
